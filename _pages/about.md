I am an applied mathematician interested in modeling complex systems. As of 2026, I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.math.psu.edu/) at [Penn State University](https://www.psu.edu/). I am also affiliated with the [Pritchard Laboratory](https://sites.psu.edu/fluidslab/) and the [Center for Computational Mathematics and Applications](https://www.ccma.psu.edu/).

I am actively recruiting students and postdocs for my group. Please feel free to reach out if you are looking for research opportunities in the following areas 

- Data-driven modeling
- Applied dynamical systems
- Active matter
- Fluid instabilities
- Granular mechanics
- Complex networks
